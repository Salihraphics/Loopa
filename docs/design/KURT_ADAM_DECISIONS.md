# Kurt Adam: decisions for the open points (spec section 9)

Decided by the product owner on 2026-11-14. These complete the master specification and are
authoritative.

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Role distribution | 6–8 players: 1 wolf, 0 independent · 9–11: 2 wolves, 1 independent · 12–15: 3 wolves, 1 independent · 16–20: 4 wolves, 2 independent. Everyone else is village-aligned, with at least one plain Köylü. Validated before the start; never more roles than players. |
| 2 | Suikastçı | Only from 10 players. (At 9 players the single independent can only be Soytarı.) |
| 3 | Kâhin | May inspect the same player on consecutive nights. |
| 4 | Cadı | May not use both potions on the same player. |
| 5, 7, 9 | Night order | 1) Susturma 2) Protections (Doktor, Cadı protection, Guardian) 3) Attacks (wolves, Brute, Suikastçı, Cadı poison) 4) Kurt Yavrusu last move (protections do not apply) 5) Investigations. Avcı arrow: stopped only by Guardian taking it; Doktor / Cadı protection do not stop it. |
| 6 | Medyum | Cannot revive a player who was eliminated in the same night. |
| 8 | Illusionist | Follows the spec: the same false role is shown to every investigator that night. (Kâhin sees "village" for any village role; Dedektif compares using the false role's team.) |
| 10 | Independents and winning | Soytarı eliminated by the day vote wins alone at once. Suikastçı wins if they are the last one alive. A village or wolf victory is not declared while the Suikastçı is alive. |
